# Contact us

To start a new project you can get in touch with by emailing us at bioinfo-facility@upsc.se or contact Edoardo Piombo directly at edoardo.piombo@umu.se.

For people who have started a project or use any of the microscopy connected computers, ask us to be added to our Slack channel as well as mailing list. These are the two channels we use to communicate about server updates and downtime (as well as other technical issues), but also those we use to discuss projects, provide support, *etc.*
